# No AI Slop Skill

Scans input text for AI tropes, buzzwords, and structural patterns, and rewrites it in clear, natural human prose.

## Pattern Dictionary (What to Detect & Cut)
1. **Throat-Clearing Openers**:
   - "In today's fast-paced world..."
   - "In the ever-evolving landscape of..."
   - "It goes without saying that..."
2. **Hype Words & Overused Adjectives**:
   - "game-changing", "revolutionary", "groundbreaking", "cutting-edge", "next-level", "seamless".
3. **Overused Verbs & Nouns**:
   - "delve", "foster", "spearhead", "navigate", "unlock", "testament", "tapestry".
4. **Pretentious Passive Voice**:
   - "It is essential to remember that..." -> State the rule directly.
5. **Generic Conclusions**:
   - "In conclusion, whether you are a beginner or experienced..." -> Cut entirely.

## Execution Rules
- **Highlight**: Point out the detected clichés.
- **Rewrite**: Provide a direct, punchy, active-voice version.
- **Conciseness**: Target 30-50% word count reduction without losing meaning.
