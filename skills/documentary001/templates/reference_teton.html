FORENSIC VERDICT & DAM SAFETY REFORM

PRIMARY CAUSE OF FAILURE

Internal Piping Across Rock Joints

Core silt piped into unsealed rock joints under high head; single-row grout curtain failed.

MODERN ENGINEERING MANDATE

Mandatory Defensive Filter Design

Prompted strict filter criteria worldwide. Cores are never placed against open rock without downstream chimney filters.
